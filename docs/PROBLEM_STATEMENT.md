# SIH 2026 — Problem Statement SIH26265

**Title:** Streamline of security deposit/ FDR portal
**Organization:** AICTE · **Department:** Regulation Bureau · **Category:** Software · **Theme:** Smart Automation
**Idea submission deadline:** 28 October 2026
**Source:** https://sih.gov.in/sih2026PS (no dataset, no video, no contact given)

## Background
AICTE (Ministry of Education) runs approval-related regulatory work for technical institutions under its regulations, Approval Process Handbooks and administrative procedures. Depending on the requirements, institutions may have to submit or maintain **security deposits or Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs)** as part of approval and compliance.

Managing them means keeping institution-wise records, verifying the instruments, watching validity and maturity dates, tracking renewal or replacement, recording release/refund requests, and keeping documents available for verification. With fragmented records, manual correspondence and several tracking mechanisms, it is hard to keep an accurate, current view of deposit status, pending actions and deadlines.

AICTE needs a centralised, streamlined portal that cuts repetitive admin work, improves record accuracy, sends timely alerts to officials and institutions, tracks each deposit transparently through its lifecycle, and follows AICTE requirements and authorised financial procedures.

## Detailed description
A secure, centralised **Security Deposit/FDR Management Portal** for the AICTE Regulation Bureau and other authorised stakeholders:

- **Records** — institution-wise and instrument-wise: reference numbers, issuing bank, amount, issue date, maturity date, validity status, supporting documents, current administrative status.
- **Documents** — digital submission and storage of FDRs and other prescribed records.
- **Verification** — officials review submissions, verify instrument details through legitimate bank-issued verification mechanisms where supported, find incomplete records and flag discrepancies. The system must **distinguish data submitted by the institution from data independently confirmed by an authorised bank or financial source.**
- **Monitoring** — track maturity dates, validity periods, renewal/replacement requirements and other timelines; configurable reminders before deadlines; overdue/unresolved cases flagged for follow-up.
- **Release / discharge / refund** — record and track requests, subject to regulations, prescribed conditions and competent-authority authorisation.
- **Dashboard** — active, expiring, matured, renewed, released and pending instruments. Users can search, review document histories, update permitted fields, record scrutiny observations and monitor each case.
- **Reports** — pending verifications, upcoming maturities, missing documents, cases needing intervention.
- **Security** — role-based access, secure document storage, encryption, audit trails, controlled access to financial and institutional data.
- **Integration** — where feasible and formally authorised, with existing AICTE systems and bank verification services, to avoid duplicate entry and improve reliability.

## Expected solution — 10 capabilities
1. **Centralized Security Deposit/FDR Repository** — institution-wise and instrument-wise records, documents and status in one portal.
2. **Digital Document Management** — secure upload, storage, retrieval and review of FDRs, deposit confirmations and other records.
3. **Verification and Discrepancy Detection** — verify through authorised sources where available; flag missing information, inconsistencies or documents needing scrutiny.
4. **Automated Validity and Maturity Tracking** — monitor dates; advance reminders for expiring/maturing instruments and renewal/replacement actions.
5. **Renewal and Replacement Monitoring** — track renewed/replacement submissions with a complete history of changes.
6. **Release and Refund Workflow** — submission, processing and status tracking of release/refund requests per authorised procedures.
7. **Automated Alerts and Escalation** — notify stakeholders of pending verification, approaching deadlines, overdue actions, unresolved cases.
8. **Dashboard and Management Reports** — consolidated view of active, expiring, matured, renewed, released and pending deposits/FDRs.
9. **Audit Trail and Access Control** — traceable record of submissions, verification results, changes, approvals and actions.
10. **System Integration and Data Security** — authorised integration with existing systems; protect confidential financial and institutional data.

## Evaluation hints in the statement
- **Measurable improvements** to demonstrate: record accuracy, verification turnaround time, timely renewal monitoring, reduction in manual tracking effort, visibility into pending cases.
- **Test scenarios** to cover: new FDR submission, validity tracking, approaching maturity, renewal, discrepancy identification, authorised release processing.

## Target users
AICTE Regulation Bureau officials, other authorised stakeholders (competent authority, accounts/finance), and institutions submitting deposits.
